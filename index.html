Formatting: a blank line starts a new paragraph. A line beginning with // becomes a dim stage cue — it is displayed but skipped by the voice tracker, so you can write // pause, look at camera without the prompter waiting to hear it.

Setup

The laptop preset pulls the text into a narrow column high on the screen and shows only a few lines at a time, so your eyes stay close to the lens instead of travelling down the display.

Your current look is remembered on its own and comes back when you reopen the page. Saved setups are for keeping more than one — a tablet rig and a laptop rig, say — and switching between them in a tap.

On the spare tablet open this same address with remote.html on the end, and enter the same pair code. Both devices need internet. Only the pair code, your position and a few words around it travel over the link — turn off Send text to remote and nothing from your script leaves this device.

Use the timed roll if speech recognition is unavailable or you are reading something the tracker struggles with. It scrolls at a constant rate and ignores the microphone.

Bluetooth remote / foot pedal: most page-turners send arrow or space keys. Space listen on/off · ↓ forward a line · ↑ back a line · M mirror · F fullscreen · Esc show the bar.
